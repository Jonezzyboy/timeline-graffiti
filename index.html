Timeline Graffiti

Type a message, open your browser's performance profiler, reload, and the message shows up in the timeline, drawn with timer chips.

How to see it

Type a message and press Paint it. The page reloads with ?text=… in the address bar. Every browser below starts drawing 1 second after load and finishes about a few seconds in, so make sure your recording runs that long.

Safari

  1. If there's no Develop menu, turn on Settings → Advanced → Show features for web developers.
  2. Open Web Inspector with ⌥⌘I and go to the Timelines tab. Untick Stop recording once page loads.
  3. Press ⌘R. Recording starts with the reload. Wait, then click the red ● to stop.
  4. In the Overview panel, keep Group By Resource ticked and click the small ▶ triangle on the first row (this page) to expand it.
  5. The five row.js?row=0…4 rows spell out your message. Zoom the ruler if the chips run together.

Chrome / Edge

  1. Open DevTools with ⌥⌘I and go to the Performance panel.
  2. Click Record and reload (the ⟳ button). Wait, then click Stop.
  3. Find the Timeline Graffiti track group and expand it. Its rows row 0…4 spell out your message.

Firefox

  1. Open DevTools with ⌥⌘I and go to the Performance panel, then click Start recording.
  2. Reload the page with ⌘R. Wait, then click Capture recording. The Firefox Profiler opens.
  3. Open the Marker Chart tab and search for graffiti. The rows graffiti 0…4 spell out your message.

Each pixel row is loaded as its own script. Every lit pixel is a setTimeout, which Safari files under that script, plus a performance.measure, which Chrome and Firefox draw on a row of its own. · jonezzyboy*
